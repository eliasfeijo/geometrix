# Pillars

A filter for every design decision: a feature that serves no pillar needs a good justification.

1. **The war is won in the macro; the battle, in the micro.** Economy, logistics, building and diplomacy decide the
   match. Real-time combat wins clashes, not the war.
2. **Wealth is physical.** Every resource and currency exists somewhere on the map and has to be carried to the base,
   by creeps or mules. On the way, it can be stolen. *(Proposal: this is the link between the world and the battles.)*
3. **Any creep can be a hero.** The player has no fixed hero: they *dominate* an allied creep, control it and make it
   grow, and then release it.
4. **The world does not wait for you.** A match lasts days. While you are away, the autopilot settings you left apply.
5. **Collective decisions have rules.** The team governs itself: votes, authority levels, taxes, public treasury,
   representatives. *(Governance is parked, [GDD 04](gdd/04-governance.md).)*
6. **Geometry is the visual language.** Shape communicates role (square = melee tank, circle = ranged glass cannon,
   triangle = middle ground at medium range). SVG and shapes instead of raster art.

## Non-goals

> Proposal, to validate with the owner.

- Not a LoL/Dota clone, and it does not need to stick to the "MOBA" concept.
- It does not require humans to be online all the time.
- Not a generic engine. Reusable parts may be extracted later, if a real common problem shows up.
- Not, in v1, a reflex-based e-sport competitive game.
